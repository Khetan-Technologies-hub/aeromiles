## Quality Assurance & Compliance

In defence operations, reliability is non-negotiable. Every Aeromiles system undergoes a stringent validation process to ensure peak performance in extreme environments.

### Compliance Standards:
- **Environmental Stress Testing:** Validation across extreme temperature ranges and high-humidity conditions.
- **Fail-Safe Redundancy:** Triple-redundant flight controllers and emergency return-to-home (RTH) protocols.
- **Regulatory Alignment:** Adherence to national aviation authority guidelines for beyond-visual-line-of-sight (BVLOS) operations.
- **Strict Quality Control:** Component-level traceability and rigorous pre-flight certification for every unit.
